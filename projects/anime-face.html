how it works

1. landmarks + background

MediaPipe's face landmarker finds 478 points on the face. The photo is lined up so the eyes sit in fixed spots. A second small model separates you from the background so it can be swapped for a drawn sky.

2. anime proportions

The points are pushed toward anime proportions: eyes grow (taller more than wider), the nose and mouth shrink, the lower face shortens, and the jaw narrows more toward the chin into a V. The photo is split into about a thousand triangles and each one is warped onto the new shape.

3. cel shading

An edge-preserving blur flattens skin and hair without smudging edges. Brightness is then snapped into a few flat bands (color bands), lifted and made more saturated.

4. ink lines

A difference-of-Gaussians edge detector (XDoG) finds outlines and draws them in dark ink. Flat areas stay clean.

5. drawn eyes + blush

Each eye opening is traced from the eyelid landmarks. A big iris is painted inside it: a gradient, a pupil, a lid shadow and two highlights, colored from your real iris. Then a thick lash line with a flick, a crease and blush strokes.
