Face Merge

Drop in photos of two different people and see their faces merged into one. It finds 478 points on each face, lines the faces up, averages their shape, warps both photos onto that shape, and blends them.

everything runs in your browser — photos are never uploaded or stored.

how it works

1. landmarks

MediaPipe's face landmarker finds 478 points on each face: eyes, brows, nose, lips, jaw and face outline.

2. align

Each photo is rotated, scaled and shifted so both pairs of eyes land in the same spots on a 512×512 frame.

3. average + triangulate

The target shape is a weighted average of the two point sets. A Delaunay triangulation splits it into about a thousand triangles.

4. warp + blend

Every triangle in each photo gets its own affine transform onto the matching target triangle. The two warped photos are then cross-faded. The shape slider sets the geometry and blend sets the skin and color, so you can put A's skin on B's face shape.
